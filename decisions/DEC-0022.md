# DEC-0022: Optional `/auto` Backlog-Drain Mode

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Users want `/auto` to continue across already planned stories until completion
without repeated manual re-invocation, while preserving decision-gate safety and
deterministic stop behavior.

Current `/auto` behavior is single-segment continuation and does not provide
explicit multi-story bounded orchestration switches.

## Decision

Add optional backlog-drain controls to `/auto`:

- `AUTO_BACKLOG_DRAIN=0|1` (default `0`)
- `AUTO_BACKLOG_MAX_STORIES>=1` (default `1`)
- `AUTO_BACKLOG_ON_BLOCK=stop|skip` (default `stop`)
- `AUTO_STORY_SELECTION=priority_then_backlog_order` (default)

Behavior:

- With drain disabled (`0`), preserve current single-segment continuation.
- With drain enabled (`1`), iterate story-by-story through full lifecycle
  deterministically until bounded stop criteria.
- Decision gates remain mandatory and pause progression.
- Blocked-story behavior follows explicit `stop|skip` policy and is logged with
  reason codes.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Enables unattended multi-story progression where safe and desired.
- Preserves existing safe default behavior for teams not using drain mode.
- Improves auditability with per-story backlog-drain breadcrumbs.

### Negative

- Adds orchestration complexity and additional reason-code/test coverage.
- Requires careful deterministic story selection to avoid ambiguity.
