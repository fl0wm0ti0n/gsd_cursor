# DEC-0025: Canonical Story Status Ownership and Drift Guard

- Date: 2026-03-01
- Status: Accepted
- Related story: US-0045
- Research refs: R-0009, R-0014

## Context

Story status drift has occurred across `docs/product/backlog.md`,
`docs/product/acceptance.md`, and `docs/engineering/state.md`, causing
automation confusion (stories implemented/released but still marked OPEN in
non-canonical views).

## Decision

1. Canonical owner for story status (`OPEN|DONE`) is
   `docs/product/backlog.md`.
2. `docs/product/acceptance.md` and `docs/engineering/state.md` are derived
   views reconciled at deterministic boundaries.
3. Reconciliation is target-scoped only and must not broad-rewrite unrelated
   stories/sprints.
4. Historical drift is repaired through a one-time normalization baseline
   documented in `docs/engineering/status-normalization-report.md`.
5. Contradictions at release/reconciliation boundaries fail safe with
   deterministic reason code `CANONICAL_STATUS_CONFLICT` (alongside
   `BACKLOG_STATUS_DRIFT` where applicable).

## Consequences

- Status authority becomes predictable and auditable.
- Existing drift is repaired with a durable baseline report.
- Future workflow runs remain deterministic with explicit failure semantics.
- Derived artifacts can still provide useful visibility without acting as
  competing status authorities.
