# DEC-0042: Deterministic State Hot-Surface Rollover Enforcement

- Date: 2026-03-14
- Status: Accepted
- Related story: US-0060
- Research refs: R-0036

## Context

`docs/engineering/state.md` grows rapidly in active repos because compaction was
defined as policy but not strictly enforced by deterministic rollover triggers.

## Decision

1. Add deterministic rollover triggers for `state.md` hot surface:
   - `STATE_HOT_MAX_LINES` (default `1200`)
   - `STATE_HOT_MAX_CHECKPOINTS` (default `80`)
2. Enforce rollover during `/refresh-context` when either threshold is exceeded.
3. Archive older checkpoints into deterministic packs under
   `docs/engineering/state-archive/` with non-destructive evidence preservation.
4. Keep bounded recent checkpoints in hot surface.
5. Fail safe with deterministic reason codes on rollover ambiguity or archive
   persistence failures:
   - `STATE_ARCHIVE_BOUNDARY_AMBIGUOUS`
   - `STATE_ARCHIVE_WRITE_FAILED`
6. Require active/template parity and regression coverage for threshold crossing,
   idempotent reruns, and fail-safe behavior.

## Consequences

- `state.md` remains bounded and usable as hot context.
- Historical evidence remains preserved in deterministic archive packs.
- Operators receive explicit diagnostics instead of silent growth drift.
