# DEC-0033: Intake Decomposition Heuristics and Risk-Aware Questioning

- Date: 2026-03-11
- Status: Accepted
- Related story: US-0051
- Research refs: R-0024, R-0025

## Context

Broad intake requests can collapse into one oversized story with many ACs,
reducing sprintability and traceability. Guided questioning currently emphasizes
ambiguity, which can under-question broad/high-impact requests that still appear
concrete.

## Decision

1. Add deterministic intake decomposition heuristics that evaluate breadth/risk
   and propose multi-story output when thresholds are exceeded.
2. Use vertical-slice/workflow-step split strategy to produce independently
   testable, user-valuable stories (avoid technical-layer-only splits).
3. Persist split rationale and boundaries in product artifacts and PO->TL handoff.
4. Extend guided intake questions with risk-aware triggers (in addition to
   ambiguity triggers), while keeping bounded question rounds.
5. Preserve user authority to accept, adjust, or merge proposed split output
   before persistence.
6. Keep low-touch mode semantics (`INTAKE_GUIDED_MODE=0`) with baseline duplicate
   safety and no forced proactive depth.

## Consequences

- Intake quality and planning readiness improve for broad requests.
- Behavior remains bounded and controllable (no open-ended interviews).
- Additional heuristics and documentation are required to avoid over-splitting.
